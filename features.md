# Features.md

## Original idea (as written)

An app with an AI model connected to your camera, showing you how to do specific yoyo tricks, from simple ones to harder, more complex combos.

Workflow for a trick *x*: search YouTube for "x yoyo tutorial", find good quality videos and their transcripts, put them in a folder, analyse how the trick works, then take the advice they give on making it easier.

What it tells you: a rating out of ten (don't be sycophantic in this), what you did right, and what you did wrong / could improve on.

## Camera coaching

* Point the camera at yourself and throw. The app watches, and tells you what happened.
* It should know *which* trick you were attempting, and follow you through the steps of it.
* It should say where you failed, not just that you failed — "you failed at step 4, because your yoyo hand was too close to your body."
* Feedback has to be honest. A 4 out of 10 means "occasionally lands with a consistent bias", not "good effort". An app that flatters you is worthless for practice.

## Trick knowledge base

* Built from real tutorials, not invented. Every piece of advice traceable to a named source video.
* Should cover beginner tricks through to hard combos.
* Should know the prerequisites of each trick, so it can tell you what to learn first.
* Should know the *common mistakes* instructors call out, because those are what it will diagnose in your footage.

## What it should tell you

* A rating out of ten, scored against the trick's rubric.
* What you did right.
* What you did wrong and how to fix it, in the instructor's own words where possible.
* What to practise next.

## Later / maybes

* Progress tracking and streaks, so you can see yourself getting better.
* A trick ladder — the community already grades tricks from 1 to 9, so use that.
* Other skill toys (juggling, kendama, diabolo) — the same idea mostly carries over, and the yoyo-only market is small.
* Two-handed styles (2A, 3A) and offstring (4A) and counterweight (5A). Much harder to see on camera; not for v1.
