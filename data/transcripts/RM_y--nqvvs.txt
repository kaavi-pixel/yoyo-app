So you’ve mastered the Breakaway from our previous video, and now you are interested in learning Barrel Rolls, because, well, they’re awesome, but you know you need to learn how to do Man on the Flying Trapeze first and so you’re wondering, “Can I teach you?” You better believe it, so let’s do that now. The first thing that you to do is throw your breakaway as I already said. Next you are going to take your opposite hand first finger and just set it here and you don’t want to move it. When you throw your breakaway the first thing you want to do is move your hand toward the yoyo which will slow it down. Then you are going to move it away from this first finger which will accelerate the yoyo over that finger and hopefully onto the string. Make sure when you attempt to land it on the string you try to land it very close to this finger, because if you land it far away it is very easy to miss. If you do miss the string with the yoyo on the outside of the string that means your yoyo hand is too close to your body. If you miss with the yoyo on the inside of your string that means your yoyo hand is too far away from your body. So as long as you keep both hands aligned you will hit it every time.
