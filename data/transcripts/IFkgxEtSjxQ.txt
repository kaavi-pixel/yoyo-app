this yo-yo trick is the sleeper here's how you do it start by holding the yo-yo with your palm up make sure that the string is wound over the top of the yo-yo if the string is wound under the yo-yo it won't roll off properly hold the yo-yo beside your ear and make a muscle to throw extend your arm and let the yo-yo roll off your hand avoid prematurely tugging your throw hand upwards as this can cause the yo-yo to unintentionally shoot back when the yo-yo gets to the bottom of the string flip your hand down and give it a tug with your wrist and catch the yo-yo back ensure you master the sleeper because this is going to be foundational for every other trick
