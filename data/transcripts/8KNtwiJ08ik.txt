The next trick I have for you today is called the Barrel Rolls. Like the Brain Twister, you mount it the same way, except you add a couple moves into the element. So you mount the yo-yo, same way, stick your right hand into the string, right in the middle, and you want to hop the yo-yo over your fingers. First over your right finger, then you follow through with your left finger. Right, left, right, left. And you just repeat the trick. To dismount, you pretty much just take off, take out your right index finger, and you're put back into the same mount. And you could just dismount it the same way, like that. And that's the Barrel Roll.
